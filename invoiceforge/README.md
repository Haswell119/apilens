# InvoiceForge — Free Invoice Generator (print to PDF, no signup)

**InvoiceForge** turns a blank page into a client-ready invoice in about a minute. Fill in
your business details, line items, tax and currency; the totals calculate themselves; print
to PDF. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — your data stays in the browser. Built for freelancers,
solo consultants, and tradespeople who currently format invoices in Google Docs or Word.

> Live app: **https://invoiceforge-od38.onrender.com** — free to use, no sign-up required.
> Pro: **$19 one-time** (lifetime) — removes the "Made with InvoiceForge" credit and adds
> save/reload of your invoices and saved templates.
> Built and operated by **Meridian Digital**.

## What InvoiceForge does

- **Line items that compute** — quantities, rates, tax and discounts total themselves, so
  disputes get caught at the line-item level before they happen.
- **Print-to-PDF invoice** — a clean, professional layout your client can actually read.
- **Due date & late-fee calculator** — pick Net 7/14/15/30/60/90 or custom terms and see the
  exact due date plus late-payment interest.
- **Private by design** — everything runs in the browser; no account, no server upload.
