# HostLens — Vacation-Rental Review Analytics

**HostLens** turns a host's own Airbnb / VRBO review export into an instant analytics
dashboard: rating trends over time, recurring-complaint detection, and ready-to-send
response drafts — so property managers with 10+ listings stop guessing what guests
complain about and start fixing it.

> Live app: **https://hostlens-nd5h.onrender.com** — free tools, no sign-up required.
> Paid plan: $19/mo (review analytics for the whole portfolio).
> Built and operated by **Meridian Digital**.

## What HostLens does

- **Rating trends** — chart your star ratings across listings and over time; spot a
  listing drifting downward before it costs you bookings.
- **Recurring-complaint detection** — the engine clusters reviews by theme
  (cleanliness, WiFi, noise, check-in, communication, bed comfort, amenities, location,
  value) and flags the complaints that keep coming back.
- **Response drafts** — deterministic, theme-aware reply drafts in 3 tones
  (professional / warm / concise) for the review you just received.

## Free tools (no account, no cost)

- **[Response generator](https://hostlens-nd5h.onrender.com/response-generator)** — paste
  one review, get a themed reply draft in seconds. The fastest way to answer a negative
  Airbnb review.
- **[Review-response template library](https://hostlens-nd5h.onrender.com/templates)** —
  11 copy-paste response examples for every common complaint theme (cleanliness, WiFi,
  noise, check-in, and more).
- **[CSV analyzer](https://hostlens-nd5h.onrender.com)** — upload your own review export
  (CSV) for a free theme breakdown. Import-first by design: you export from Airbnb/VRBO
  yourself, so there is no scraping and no ToS risk.
